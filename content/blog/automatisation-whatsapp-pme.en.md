WhatsApp has become the preferred contact channel for SMEs in Morocco, both for customers and prospects. The problem: answering every message manually doesn't scale once volume grows.

## What a well-designed WhatsApp AI agent does

It answers frequently asked questions instantly (hours, pricing, availability). It asks the right questions to qualify the prospect (budget, need, timeline). It automatically hands off qualified leads to a salesperson, with the conversation context included. It stays available 24/7, including weekends.

## What it doesn't replace

An AI agent isn't designed to close a complex sale or handle a sensitive complaint. These situations should be routed to a human, with the conversation context already available so nothing has to be repeated.

## A realistic rollout

At Essoria, a simple WhatsApp automation (FAQ + qualification) typically deploys in one to two weeks. More advanced scenarios (CRM integration, multilingual support) are added afterward, once the foundation works well.

The goal is never to replace your sales team, but to hand it only prospects who are already qualified and interested.
